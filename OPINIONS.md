# What Justin thinks (by topic)

Each point links to the public post, video or page it comes from and ends with the date of its newest supporting quote. Where his position is contested or has shifted, both sides are kept, each dated, and say which is newer.

## Learning vs studying
- Studying is the activity; learning is the outcome. "studying does not equal learning you can spend a lot of time studying and get very little learning out of it" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HrPE3Zmq0mk)). Learning only counts if you can remember it, understand it deeply and apply it. (2024)
- Effort is the point, not a bug. "don't try to make Learning Easy make learning harder" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goHaIfMcfWo)). People quit good methods because "when it doesn't feel easy, you're doing something wrong" (the misinterpreted effort hypothesis, [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTRMX2heR5k), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whlrIqXcTKo)). (2026)
- Confusion is the signal you're learning: "Confusion is the symptom of learning… If you don't feel confusion, your brain is not figuring anything out." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nIABz0Z4IRA)) "Instead, you just turn that confusion into a question." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R7jC6TUB8P4)) (2026)
- Stop judging methods by speed. Volume covered is "one of the easiest metrics to measure. It's also one of the least accurate." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R7jC6TUB8P4)) Real efficiency is "how long does it take in total to reach the level you need to reach" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goHaIfMcfWo)), and dropping the speed obsession was one of my biggest upgrades ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CQQTwvDb5xg)). (2026)
- Higher-order thinking beats repetition: grouping, relating, comparing, finding structure. Good thinkers are fast because "they're willing to be wrong more frequently" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQHA7EYq8sw)). "An analogy is like a bridge that connects new information to your existing knowledge." ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1729802411626426728)) (2026)

## Techniques, systems and notes
- There's no single best technique. "I don't think there's anything as a best learning strategy for every single context" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nIABz0Z4IRA)). If someone asks for the best technique, they don't yet see "techniques rather than systems" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6GTt10GDWII)). Check every technique's intended vs actual effect ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1832727916696002998)). (2026)
- Highlighting is mostly wasted time ("I've wasted hundreds of hours of my life… just highlighting things", [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTRMX2heR5k)) and is often "an emotional coping strategy" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTRMX2heR5k)). (2026)
- Notes serve learning, not the other way round: "Don't be a slave to your notes." ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1252785082831519744), 2020). With AI transcription you rarely need your own linear notes ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CQQTwvDb5xg)). Non-linear notes transform the experience ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1729424996353892740)). (2026)
- Thinking on paper "is probably the most important skill that you can learn" to avoid the common learning red flags ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R7jC6TUB8P4)). (2026)
- Memory isn't memorization: "memory and memorization are not the same thing" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-gqYldhjZVk)). Filter first: decide "what you should and should not memorize" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-gqYldhjZVk)). You "can't retrieve what you don't encode" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--Hu2w0s72Y)). (2026)
- **Shifted: flashcards / active recall.** In 2021 I said if you think active recall and spaced repetition work best, "you're probably wrong" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=--Hu2w0s72Y), 2021-10). By 2023 I said "I don't hate flash cards", called them "an incredibly important and effective tool… when you're using it the right way" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WkjVaMDheJ4), 2023-12), and said I regret hating on them in that earlier video ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WkjVaMDheJ4), 2023-12). In 2026 I still put a memorization filter first ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-gqYldhjZVk), 2026-10). The 2023–2026 view is newer: flashcards are fine for the right job, after real encoding. (newest: 2026)
- **Shifted: mind maps.** 2020: "i haven't really found a situation where it hasn't been effective" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5zT_2aBP6vM), 2020-07). 2022: "mind maps most of the time do not work", and I explained why most attempts fail ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHAhV8YIlks), 2022-09). 2026: "there are very few other techniques that when done properly are as useful as mind mapping." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_tuS9sWJEk), 2026-07) Newest view: very powerful, but only done properly, and most people hit the same problem first. (newest: 2026)
- Learning styles are a myth with a useful kernel: "even though learning styles don't exist, it's still matters" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qOjSJVaBV94)). Believing in them "creates this artificial limit" ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1737035113307996227)). "you should learn to use multiple modalities" ([The Futur](https://thefutur.com/podcast/justin-sung-learning-beyond-studying), [The Futur](https://thefutur.com/podcast/justin-sung-learning-beyond-studying)). (2026)

## Learner development
- Most people need to learn to learn, and "by the time you realize you need to start learning to learn, it's already too late." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MMACQZBC860)) (2025)
- Natural high achievers often succeed "despite their techniques not because of them" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a620mE9nyBQ)). What separates learners is deep processing, self-regulation and mindset, all trainable: "both self-regulation and deep processing require practice and time to train" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a620mE9nyBQ)). Telling someone mindset is the problem without tools "actually makes things worse" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dYRmZdwi9mo)). (2025)
- Hard work alone doesn't beat talent: "the formula for success is not simply hard work times Talent there's a missing ingredient": process ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aRHTEhQp1g0)). (2023)
- Treat every "silly mistake" as a real knowledge gap ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HrPE3Zmq0mk)). Change one thing at a time ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goHaIfMcfWo)). Cram early, not late ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=goHaIfMcfWo)). Watching videos isn't practice: "it's a skill it's not just a theory" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eauQac_23R0)). (2025)
- Boredom is a skill problem: "I found learning so boring back then was because I just sucked at learning." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6U8zNlqCa6M)) (2025)

## AI and learning
- AI is here and it's changing learning: "AI is revolutionizing learning. That's a fact. At this point, there is no going back." It's also "not the savior" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gQIAXjraLo)). (2026)
- Used wrong, it makes you dumber: "If you've been using ChatBT to learn for the last year, you are probably dumber today than you were a year ago." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sJ50Ybp44I), 2025). It raises the value of expertise, it doesn't lower it ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sJ50Ybp44I)). (2025)
- My LLM flowchart: if I don't know and it doesn't matter, fine, use it ("don't know, doesn't matter", [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFtB-ekr8d4)). If I don't know but it matters, I can't judge the output or even prompt well ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFtB-ekr8d4)). The goal is to be "AI-enhanced rather than AI-dependent" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFtB-ekr8d4)). (2026)
- Career angle: "you do not need to become an AI expert to remain competitive. You just need to be good at what AI is not good at." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Xc_ExgwLs8)) The race is whether you learn faster than AI does ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Xc_ExgwLs8)). People will lose jobs, "And I don't take any joy in in predicting this" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vBftJEuppKM)). (2026)
- Study mode is good on balance; one upside is "you can feel free to be dumb" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m3jNwwuvqx8)). (2025)
- **How my view moved:** 2022, excited by new AI tools ("really really exciting", [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7fFqdsuof0g), 2022-10). 2023: for excellence "it's not there yet" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0bHMsDlTmE), 2023-04). 2025–26: revolutionary but risky, use it deliberately ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sJ50Ybp44I), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4gQIAXjraLo), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yFtB-ekr8d4)). Newest view is the 2026 one. (newest: 2026)

## Education and edtech
- "I think that modern education is failing the next generation." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umVX5wK6OZc)) Edtech is hard to build well ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umVX5wK6OZc)): if a product makes learners do the effortful part, "they are punished by being forced to do something that's effortful" and engagement drops ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umVX5wK6OZc)). (2026)
- **Contested: black and white thinking.** In 2025 I wrote that seeing things "as “black and white” is a red flag for overconfidence" and the value is "in understanding the grey" ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1946782726201147782), [X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1946782726201147782), 2025-07). In Oct 2026, on kids and devices, I said I usually avoid it but "we really need to be thinking about this in a black and white way" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=umVX5wK6OZc), 2026-10). The newer line is narrower: an exception for digital tech in schools, not a general rule. (newest: 2026)
- Textbooks are "honestly kind of a scam" on price ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1E9icvNR4TA)). (2023)
- Better teaching needs better systems, not just better people: "Expecting better teachers is like expecting better doctors. There's a reason it doesn't work" ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1423264388182515712)). Coaches should be trained to a "#medical standard" ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1521630972667920384)). (2022)
- Most study advice doesn't hold up: "99% of the study and learning advice I've seen… simply do NOT hold up in real life." ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1257493261066727424), [X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1263628924350652417)) (2020)

## Productivity, procrastination, energy
- "Prioritisation is the most high leverage productivity skill you can master." ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1945539187383701732)) If there's too much to learn, "Spend less time learning" and sharpen the axe ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1945608809780842914), [X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1945608809780842914)). Spend effort strategically to avoid overwhelm ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1750117010283913685)). (2025)
- Procrastination "is not a single behavior" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Jpx6eNfpoo)). It's the "emotional toll of a task exceeding our tolerance" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Jpx6eNfpoo)). Find the cause, then the fix. (2026)
- "don't trust your your brain" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-aEFft14VI)): "your brain is not there to help you reach your productivity goals your brain is just there to keep you alive" ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-aEFft14VI)). Expect it to slip and plan for that. (2025)
- Burnout: a break isn't automatically the answer ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1vPAYLYLbMQ), 2022). Sustainable output beats raw effort: "optimum output comes from the optimal person" ([X](https://x.com/DrJustinSung/status/1246593125402861568), 2020). (2022)
- Sleep is non-negotiable: "please don't sacrifice sleep. And anyone that tells you to sacrifice sleep, just unfollow them, please." ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=whlrIqXcTKo)) I learned this the hard way ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ernU9Vk6y0)). (2026)
- Commit or quit is a saying I "absolutely hate" because of how people read it: effort alone doesn't solve every challenge ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h8fGC0PXk4k)). (2020)
